About the Club

Naifaru Chelsea Sports Club, officially registered on 20th February 2023, is a football club dedicated to nurturing and inspiring young talent in Lh. Naifaru. The club was formed with the vision of creating a strong football culture and offering youth the opportunity to grow both on and off the field. Since its foundation, Naifaru Chelsea SC has focused on unity, discipline, and community-driven sporting excellence.
